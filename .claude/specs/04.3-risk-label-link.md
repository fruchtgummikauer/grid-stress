## Goal

Spec 04 measures SMARD's forecast on `data/smard.csv` alone. This sub-spec connects that error to
the project's own risk labels, in two steps:

1. **Error on risk hours and risk days** (regression): how large is SMARD's error on the hours and
   days the actual risk flags mark as at risk, compared with unflagged ones?
2. **Flag agreement** (classification): apply the exported risk thresholds to `fc_residual_load` and
   check whether SMARD's forecast would have flagged the same days as the actual residual load.

Both use the same label files, the same loading rules and the same staleness check, which is why
they share one sub-spec.

## Scope

### IN (when un-parked)

- **Loading the labels.** Plain CSV. Keep every flag column as a nullable boolean: an empty cell
  means **not evaluable**, never `False` — no `fillna(False)` anywhere. Fail with a clear message
  naming `notebooks/03_risk_classification/risk-definition.ipynb` if either file is missing, and fail
  if the hourly file's timestamps do not match `time_series.index` exactly (labels built from a
  different `smard.csv` snapshot are stale and must be regenerated first).
- **Error on risk hours.** Join `data/risk_classification/risk_labels_hourly.csv` and group hours by the **actual** risk
  flag, per recommended basis: `rolling` (high and low) and `zero` (low). Groups: flagged high,
  unflagged, flagged low; not-evaluable hours are **excluded and counted**, never merged into
  "unflagged". Per group: MAE, RMSE, bias, `hour_count`, via spec 04's `window_metrics` with a mask.
  No nMAE for residual load on risk hours; this extends spec 04's Behaviour 7 rule, because
  low-risk hours have a mean near or below zero.
- **Day-extreme error on risk days.** Spec 04's day-max / day-min value errors, grouped by the days
  `data/risk_classification/risk_labels_daily.csv` flags under `rolling` / `zero` × `any` (flagged vs. unflagged,
  not-evaluable excluded and counted). MAE, bias and `day_count` per group.
- **Flag agreement.** Apply the **exported** thresholds — joined on `date` from
  `data/risk_classification/risk_labels_daily.csv`, never recomputed against `fc_residual_load` — to the forecast, with
  the same day rules (`any`, `3h`) and completeness rule as `risk-definition.ipynb`. Compare the
  forecast flag with the actual flag per direction × basis × rule: confusion counts (hit, miss,
  false alarm, correct negative), precision and recall, each with its `day_count`.
- The demoted `static` basis is not used.

### OUT

- Anything spec 04 rules out.
- Plain accuracy as a headline: with roughly 1 % risk days, a forecast that never flags anything
  scores about 99 % accuracy. Report confusion counts, precision and recall instead.
- Recomputing or re-tuning the thresholds, or choosing a percentile level.
- Our own model's flags — that belongs to the modelling spec, which can reuse this sub-spec's code.

## Edge cases

- **Stale or missing risk labels.** Fail rather than join on a partial overlap.
- **Empty flags.** Not evaluable (the first 365 days under `rolling`, incomplete days). Excluded and
  counted, never treated as "not at risk".
- **Non-stationary base rate.** `risk-definition.ipynb` found the low-direction base rate strongly
  non-stationary, so precision and recall over the whole record mix very different years. Report
  them per year as well.
- **Small groups.** Risk hours are a few hundred per basis, risk days fewer; `hour_count` /
  `day_count` is shown with every number.
