## Edge cases

- **STL and the DST gaps.** `STL(period=24, ...)` assumes exactly 24 observations per day; the
  spring-DST days in the record have 23. This is the same approximation
  [02-Deep-EDA.md](02-Deep-EDA.md) notes for ACF/PACF lag counting — name it in the STL section's
  prose rather than treating the decomposition as exact on those days.
- **Longest gap-free segment, computed per series.** Behaviour 1's de-seasonalized ACF runs on
  the longest run of non-null values in *that* series, not on `time_series` as a whole —
  `grid_load` and `residual_load` may have (and are expected to have) slightly different longest
  segments even though both derive from the same download.
- **`ramp`'s expected `NaN` count is dynamic.** `.diff()` produces one leading `NaN`, plus one
  per `True` in `spans_gap`. Because the record's span (and therefore the DST-gap count) is not
  fixed (see [03.1](03.1-setup.md)), the self-check must compare against
  `time_series["spans_gap"].sum() + 1`, not a hardcoded number like the `6` used in
  [02-Deep-EDA.md](02-Deep-EDA.md)'s snapshot.
- **GW → MW / MW/h.** Every value ported from `EDA-hari.ipynb` in this section (Behaviour 3's
  ramp panel, Behaviour 4, Behaviour 5) was in GW in the source notebook; convert and relabel
  every axis — levels become MW, ramps become MW/h.
- **"MWh per hour" is just MW, written awkwardly.** An hourly `residual_load` value is a level
  reading, not a summed energy volume, so `MWh` and `MW` are the same number for it — label it
  `MW`, not `"MWh per hour"` or bare `MWh`. This is why Behaviour 3's histogram (ported from
  `EDA-simple-claude.ipynb` cell 73, which used `"MWh per hour"` before that notebook was
  corrected) is labelled `MW` here.
- **Descriptive slice.** Behaviour 5's matched-window selection is a date-based descriptive slice
  under [01-Simple-EDA.md](01-Simple-EDA.md) Behaviour 20 — it stays local to its cell and is not
  persisted onto `time_series`.
- **Deep-EDA overlap is intentional.** Behaviour 4 (ramp-by-year quantiles) sits in territory
  [02-Deep-EDA.md](02-Deep-EDA.md) will also cover; it is included here because the team tagged
  and confirmed it, and does not substitute for that spec's dedicated treatment.
- **Why the Jan–Sep bar chart is missing.** `EDA-simple-claude.ipynb` cell 61 (a bar-chart version
  of the same "matched-window year comparison" idea) is excluded because its source cell carries
  no tag — Behaviour 5's dot/range plot is the sole surviving view of this comparison, and is
  visually different (median/P10–P90 range vs. grouped bars), not a redundant second copy.
