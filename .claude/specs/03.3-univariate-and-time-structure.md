## Edge cases

- **Holidays, federal only.** `holidays.country_holidays("DE")` with no `subdiv` returns federal
  holidays only. Confirm the resulting list contains none of the state-only holidays
  (Fronleichnam, Allerheiligen, Reformationstag) — their presence would mean the call picked up a
  `subdiv` default and made the analysis depend on an arbitrary Bundesland.
- **Christmas reference-window caveat.** For the Christmas holidays, the ±14-day same-weekday
  reference window in Behaviour 6 sits partly inside the depressed Christmas period itself, so
  the measured delta understates the true effect. State this in the accompanying prose rather
  than silently reporting an understated number.
- **Descriptive slices stay local.** Behaviour 7 and 8's rank/date-based selections are
  descriptive under [01-Simple-EDA.md](01-Simple-EDA.md) Behaviour 20 — no boolean column is
  added to `time_series`, and neither slice is presented as a risk definition.
- **"MWh per hour" is just MW.** An hourly value of any `SERIES` column is a level reading, not a
  summed energy volume, so `MWh` and `MW` are the same number for it. `EDA-simple-claude.ipynb`
  cells 46 and 81 originally labelled their axes `"MWh per hour"`; that source notebook has since
  been corrected to `"MW"`, and Behaviour 2 and 8 port the corrected label — do not reintroduce
  `"MWh per hour"` if these cells are ever re-copied from an older version of the source.
- **Untagged exclusions in this notebook.** The identity-check histogram (cell 27), boxplot
  (cell 47), Jan–Sep trend bars (cell 61), `SERIES` correlation heatmap (cell 66), renewables
  scatter (cell 68) and negative-share heatmap (cell 76) are all excluded — their source cells
  carry no tag. This is a deliberate, discussed cut; do not add them back without revising this
  spec. See [03-combined-cherry-picked-eda.md](03-combined-cherry-picked-eda.md) for the full
  cross-section list.
