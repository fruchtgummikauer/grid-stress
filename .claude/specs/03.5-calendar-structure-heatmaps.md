## Goal

Merge the overlapping month/weekday/season × hour heatmaps from `EDA-hari.ipynb` and
`EDA-magc.ipynb` into one figure, and port `EDA-hari.ipynb`'s remaining tagged calendar and
single-event plots, all rebuilt against `time_series` in MWh/average MW with dynamically computed
years — none of hari's original GW units or hardcoded year lists survive.

## Behaviour

1. One merged heatmap figure, 2 rows × 3 columns: `{grid_load, residual_load}` ×
   `{month × hour, weekday × hour, season × hour}` mean level. Replaces three separate figures —
   `EDA-hari.ipynb` cell 29 (`residual_load` only, tags `keep, combine with monica's version`)
   extended with a `grid_load` row, and `EDA-magc.ipynb` cells 22 and 24 (`grid_load` and
   `residual_load` hour × weekday heatmaps, tags `keep, duplicate` / `duplicate`) dropped as
   superseded.
   - The `residual_load` row keeps hari's diverging colour scale, centred on zero and shared
     across its three panels, because `residual_load` crosses zero.
   - The `grid_load` row uses its own sequential colour scale — the two rows cannot share one
     scale, since `grid_load` is always positive and on a different level entirely.
   - Weekday axes use the project's `DAY_NAMES` (Mon…Sun) from [03.1](03.1-setup.md), not hari's
     numeric 0–6 or magc's separate full-name strings.
2. National daily rhythm: mean-by-hour lines for `grid_load`, `wind_on`, `wind_off`, `solar`,
   `residual_load` together on one axis (source: `EDA-hari.ipynb` cell 26, tags `optional,
   opener/presentation/streamlit`).
3. `grid_load` profile on national holidays vs. other days, mean by hour of day (source:
   `EDA-hari.ipynb` cell 53, tags `combine with holiday plot / results, for
   streamlit/presentation/modelling target`). Rebuilt using
   `holidays.country_holidays("DE")` instead of hari's hand-rolled Easter-based date calculation,
   for consistency with [03.3](03.3-univariate-and-time-structure.md) Behaviour 6 and so the
   notebook needs no `dateutil` import. Kept alongside that behaviour's holiday-effect table —
   this one shows the hour-of-day *shape* of the effect; that one quantifies its *size* per named
   holiday.
4. Germany football-match event study: `grid_load` around 5 fixed Euro 2024 kickoffs vs. matched
   ordinary days (source: `EDA-hari.ipynb` cell 59, tag `for streamlit/single-events`). The five
   fixture dates are historical fact, not derived from the record's span, and stay as literals.
5. 24-hour harmonic fit of the mean `residual_load` hour-of-day shape, 1/2/3-harmonic overlays
   (source: `EDA-hari.ipynb` cell 32, tag `modelling`).
6. Anchor-year comparison of the mean `residual_load` hour-of-day shape, plus a
   difference-from-baseline panel (source: `EDA-hari.ipynb` cell 38, tags `combine with daily
   national, combine with grid evolution`). Replace hari's hardcoded anchor years
   `[2019, 2022, 2025, 2026]` with a rule computed from `YEARS`: the first year, the middle year
   (by position in `YEARS`), the last **complete** year, and the current partial year if the
   record ends mid-year. The baseline for the difference panel is the first year in that
   selection.

## Data

No new persisted columns. Anchor years (Behaviour 6) are computed from `YEARS`, not stored.

## Edge cases

- **Two colour scales, not one.** `residual_load` crosses zero and needs a diverging, zero-centred
  colormap; `grid_load` is strictly positive and on a different level. This is not optional
  styling — a shared scale would make one of the two rows in Behaviour 1 unreadable.
- **GW → MWh.** Every value ported from `EDA-hari.ipynb` in this section was divided by 1000 in
  the source notebook. Remove that conversion — this notebook reports in MWh / average MW — and
  relabel every affected axis.
- **Holidays, one source of truth.** Hari's Easter-offset calculation
  (`from dateutil.easter import easter`) is a second, independent implementation of the same
  federal-holiday list [03.3](03.3-univariate-and-time-structure.md) already gets from the
  `holidays` package. Behaviour 3 uses the package, not the hand-rolled version — one source of
  truth, and no new dependency on `dateutil` (currently only a transitive dependency via pandas).
- **Dynamic anchor years.** Behaviour 6's `[2019, 2022, 2025, 2026]` was hand-picked for a
  4-anchor comparison; the replacement rule must still produce a small, readable set of anchors
  (not all of `YEARS`) while containing no literal year.
- **Preserve hari's palette.** Reuse `EDA-hari.ipynb`'s `COLORS` dict and custom colormaps for
  the plots ported from that notebook, adapted only for units and dynamic year counts — this is a
  content consolidation, not a re-design of plots that already have a considered look.
- **Deep-EDA overlap is intentional.** Behaviour 5 and 6 (harmonic fit, anchor-year day shape)
  sit in territory [02-Deep-EDA.md](02-Deep-EDA.md) will also cover. They are included here
  because the team tagged and confirmed them; their presence here does not satisfy spec 02's
  acceptance criteria when that spec is written.
